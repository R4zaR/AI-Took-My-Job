Contact Us

Email razapps.support@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can. To help us help you faster, please include:

  • Your device model and OS version (e.g. iPhone 15, iOS 18).
  • A short description of what happened, and the steps that led to it.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you have one.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I remove ads?

Any tip in the Tip Jar (from the main menu) removes ads permanently — there's no separate "remove ads" purchase. You can also support the game for free by watching an optional video; that doesn't remove ads.

I already tipped — how do I restore it on a new device?

Open Tip Jar from the main menu and tap "Already tipped? Tap here to remove ads" to restore your purchase. Purchases are tied to your App Store or Google Play account.

Where are my high scores and the leaderboard?

Your personal best, stats, trophies, and settings are saved on your device. The global leaderboard is powered by Game Center on iOS and Google Play Games on Android — tap Leaderboard from the main menu after signing in to that service. (Note: iOS and Android leaderboards are separate.)

Do I need an account to play?

No. There are no accounts or sign-ups. The game is fully playable offline.

How is my data handled?

The game has no user accounts and your progress stays on your device. The only data collected is handled by the ad and purchase services the app uses. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

The game isn't working / I found a bug

Please email us at razapps.support@gmail.com with the details listed under Contact Us above, and we'll look into it right away.
