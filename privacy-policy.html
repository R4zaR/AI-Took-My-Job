Privacy Policy

AI Took My Job · Published by RazApps · Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RazApps ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game AI Took My Job (the "App"). We built the App to respect your privacy: it has no user accounts, and your gameplay data stays on your device. The limited data described below is handled by the third-party services we use for ads and purchases.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.
  • Your game progress, high scores, statistics, achievements, settings, and chosen character are stored only on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect certain data to function:

Google AdMob (advertising)

We use Google AdMob to display ads. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (including the advertising ID), general device information, and ad-interaction data. This data is used for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud/invalid-traffic prevention, and may be shared with Google and its advertising partners. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

RevenueCat (in-app purchases)

If you make an optional in-app purchase (for example, the Tip Jar to remove ads), we use RevenueCat to process the purchase and manage your entitlement. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and an anonymous app-user identifier so we can unlock the "remove ads" benefit and restore your purchase across devices. RevenueCat acts as our service provider. We do not receive or store your payment card details — those are handled directly by the Apple App Store or Google Play. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

App Store / Google Play

All payments are processed by Apple and Google under their own terms and privacy policies. See Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

How Information Is Used

  • To display and measure advertising (via AdMob).
  • To provide app functionality, including granting and restoring the optional "remove ads" purchase (via RevenueCat).
  • For basic analytics related to ads and purchases provided by those services.

Your Choices & Ad Personalization

You can control or reset your advertising identifier and limit ad personalization at any time in your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (and Apple Advertising). The App will also ask for your tracking preference when you first play.

Purchasing the optional "remove ads" benefit stops ads from being shown in the App.

Data Retention & Deletion

Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the App. You can reset your advertising identifier using the device settings above. For any request regarding data held by our service providers, contact us at the email below and we will assist where possible.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Data Security

Data transmitted by the third-party services above is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS).

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
